fix(media): never infer a missing record from a storageKey lookup failure

Production audit proved the missing-D1-record premise was wrong: all 4,008
records exist and every migrated WordPress storage_key is registered. The 401/404
symptoms come from addressing GET /_emdash/api/media/{id} (D1 primary key) with
an R2 storageKey, and from calling the authenticated admin API without a token.

pullArticleMedia now grades each body storageKey by evidence: a record resolved
under that ULID, or a media/*.yaml companion naming it, moves the key to the new
PullMediaResult.verifiedStorageKeys. Only keys with no evidence stay in
orphanStorageKeys. New collectLocalMediaMirrorCandidates indexes companions by
record id and storageKey and excludes legacy_* residue from verification.

CLI warning renamed UNRESOLVED_MEDIA_ASSET and rewritten to stay epistemic: a 404
proves only a failed lookup, and remediation must verify by storageKey before
registering anything because a blind INSERT can duplicate. doctor wording and the
AGENTS/README/CHANGELOG/MCP guidance are aligned so nothing tells users to insert.
